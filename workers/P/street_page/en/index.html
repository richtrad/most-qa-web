Archive notes and dates

Two image records in the inventory are marked as photographs, but visual inspection shows handwritten information cards rather than photographic inputs. They were excluded from colorization. The inventory also says PHOTO,BW and has_archive_card=false; this mismatch was not edited during this preparation.

The handwritten notes contain uncertain date hints around autumn 1974 and early spring 1975. Their transcription and historical dating have not been independently verified. Both cards are included in the review handoff for visual checking.

Source and colorization scope

The input inventory has seven entries: five photographic views and two handwritten cards. Three photo groups were colorized in six attempts. Two additional photographs were skipped as near-duplicates with the same direction and geometry as cp_691_003.

Current outputs: cp_691,1894_001 · attempt 1 accepted with limitations; cp_691_003 · attempt 2 user-approved; cp_691,1894_002 · attempt 2 user-approved. Earlier records show five house attachments with verified remote bytes. Colorizations are derived images, not authentic color sources.

The phase was COLOR_ONLY: foreground objects were retained; no cleanup, object removal or hidden-surface reconstruction was done. Colors remain provisional hypotheses. This preparation did not newly audit the color database, building plans or GIS.
