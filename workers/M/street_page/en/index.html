Gen. Svobody Square

A photographic overview of role M's work to date. This page keeps archival originals distinct from provisional colour outputs and does not claim an available 3D reconstruction.

Scope and identity

What is safely documented here

The working selection contains eight separate archival photographs safely related to house no. 142 in the Gen. Svobody Square database area. Shared overlap groups and plans were excluded from the COLOR_ONLY selection; this is not an inventory of every house in the wider area.

A 3D model is not available yet. There is no viewer, render, or model download here. The missing model is not substituted with invented geometry.

Sources and colour

How to read the images

Black-and-white archival photographs document visible architecture, lettering, composition and damage; they do not, by themselves, document historical colour. The three COLOR_ONLY outputs are material references marked as provisional model hypotheses. People, vehicles, trees, wires and other foreground objects remain; cleanup and inpainting were not part of this phase.

All three outputs remain local. There is no recorded upload or remote verification.
