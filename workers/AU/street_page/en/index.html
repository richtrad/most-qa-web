The AU inventory associates 14 structure records with the Kulová area. That grouping does not prove the location or identity of any individual photographic view.

Sixteen open photographs retain the original classification 3. namesti; two more are unclassified postcards. Each of the 18 cases remains “unknown / keep blocked” until primary mapping evidence establishes the relation among image, building, and street.
